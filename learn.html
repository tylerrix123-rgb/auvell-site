What research compounds means

The phrase is used loosely online. In a laboratory setting it usually means chemical reagents supplied for in-vitro study, analytical work, or other non-clinical research. They are not the same thing as licensed medicines.

Approved medicines

Evaluated by a regulator for a stated use in people or animals. Manufactured, labeled, and distributed under drug rules.

Research reagents

Sold as chemicals for laboratory investigation. They are not approved to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease.

How to read the field

Questions worth asking

  • Is this an approved drug, a research reagent, or something being marketed as both?
  • What analytical documents exist for the specific batch?
  • Who is allowed to purchase and possess this material where you work?
  • Is the vendor describing a laboratory use, or implying a use in people?
