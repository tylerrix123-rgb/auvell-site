Auvell

A desk for the list, not a clinic.

Auvell holds a small catalogue of research compounds and answers questions about what is on that catalogue. We do not write protocols. We do not describe human use. If a pack is on the shelf, you can ask about the name, the milligrams, the lot mark, and whether it is available to talk about.

How an enquiry works

Add. Note. Message.

1

Add

Open a pack. Choose how many. That does not buy anything. It only puts the name and the milligrams on your note.

2

Note

The note is the desk. Typical listed figures sit there as context, not a bill. Write any research question you actually have.

3

Message

Send the note. The message states these are laboratory research materials, not for human or veterinary use. Then the conversation is one to one.

What this is

Short chains, held as powder.

Each pack arrives as a dry lyophilised cake in a labelled vial. Bacteriostatic water is listed on its own. Auvell will not tell you how to use a compound in a body.

What they are

Short amino-acid chains made for laboratory work. Listed by name and milligrams only.

Where they sit

Away from heat and light. Cold storage is the default until a lot file says otherwise.

What we will not do

No dosing. No stacks. No body goals. No “how to take this.” If that is the question, we will not answer it.
