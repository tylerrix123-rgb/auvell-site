How an enquiry works

Add. Note. Message.

Message

Send the note. The message states these are laboratory research materials, not for human or veterinary use. Then the conversation is one to one.

What this is

Short chains, held as powder.

A peptide research chemical is a short chain of amino acids made for laboratory work. Each one is listed by name and milligrams. Bacteriostatic water is listed on its own.

What they are

Peptide research chemicals: short amino-acid chains listed by name and milligrams only. Not a medicine. Not a supplement.

How they are kept

Capped, away from heat and light. Lyophilised powder is typically held at 2–5 °C, cold, dark and dry. After water is added, keep 2–8 °C. The lot file overrides this if it says otherwise.

What we will not do

We will not teach reconstitution. We will not describe body use, oils, stacks or dosages. We will not write a protocol or a goal. If that is the question, we will not answer it.
