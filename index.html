A short guide

What you are looking at

01

A short chain

A peptide is a short chain of amino acids. A protein is the same idea, only much longer. These vials are laboratory copies of one sequence.

02

Studied, not prescribed

Labs use them to watch how cells pass signals. This page does not say what a person should take. Not medical advice. Not for human or veterinary use.

03

Made, then dried

The powder is chemically synthesised, then dried into a small cake. It is not taken from a person or an animal.

04

Keep it sealed

Cap on. Vial upright. Dry, dark, and out of a bathroom or a sunny window. Do not open one just to look.

Storage

Where a sealed dry vial sits

General lab habit, not a promise for every compound.
−80°YearsDeep freezer. Long holding.
−20°MonthsNormal freezer. The usual place.
2–8°WeeksFridge only, cap still on.
15–25°HoursIn transit. Not overnight.
30°+AvoidHeat and sun spoil it fastest.

Store in the body of the freezer, not the door. Let a cold vial stop fogging before the cap comes off.
